# Banter's - Fool's Gambit

A Slay the Spire 2 chaos starting-relic mod.

## Fool's Gambit

Every character starts with **Fool's Gambit** instead of their normal starting relic.

At the start of the first combat, choose a Rare-card source:

- **Current Character** — Rare cards from your current character.
- **Colorless** — Rare Colorless cards.
- **Random Character** — Rare cards from one randomly selected playable character other than your current character.
- **All Card Pools** — Rare cards from every available card pool.

Then your starting deck is transformed card-for-card into random Rare cards from that source. Duplicates are allowed.

Additional effects:

- Your original Max HP is stored and your Max HP becomes **1**.
- After each combat victory, regain **10% of your original Max HP**, up to **50%** of the original value after five victories.
- Start each combat with **10 Plating**.
- At the start of every turn, **2 random cards in your hand** independently have their Energy cost randomized to **0–2** for that turn.

The intent is deliberately high variance: the transformed deck can be awful, strong, or completely broken.

Requires [BaseLib](https://github.com/Alchyr/BaseLib-StS2).
